# Kleidos Addendum to the Contributor Agreement (version 2)

This Addendum is the text of the Kleidos project, not part of the Contributor Agreements 1.2 template. English is the binding version.

## 1. This Addendum, the parties and what is covered

1.1 This Addendum is part of the Contributor Agreement (the "Agreement") and is accepted together with it in a single act. Where the two differ, this Addendum prevails. The sentence of the Agreement saying that it "sets out the entire agreement" refers to the Agreement together with this Addendum. Where the Agreement refers to its Subsection "Disclaimer of warranty", it means its Section "Disclaimer".

1.2 "We" and "Us" mean José M. Requena Plens, a natural person resident in Spain who maintains Kleidos (contact: mail@kleidos.dev), and whoever takes his place under clause 8. Kleidos is the name of the project, not a party.

1.3 "Material" means everything published in the Kleidos repositories, whatever its kind: software, firmware, documentation, hardware designs, schematics, CAD and 3D models, graphics, brand assets, media, data and website content. The Kleidos repositories are those under github.com/kleidos-dev and github.com/jmrplens/kleidos, and any repository that replaces them or is added to them.

1.4 For a Contribution that is not software, the licences of the Agreement also cover, as far as You hold them, the design rights (registered or not), the database right and the rights in semiconductor topographies embodied in the Contribution. Neither party grants a trade mark licence.

1.5 When the Agreement is accepted electronically, "You" is the holder of the GitHub account with which the acceptance is made, identified by the numeric id of that account. You accept by signing in with that account at https://cla.kleidos.dev and confirming there the acceptance sentence shown to You; if that site is unavailable, You may instead post the same sentence Yourself, from that account, as a comment on Your pull request. The signature blocks at the end of the Agreement are not filled in: Your signature is that confirmation, or that comment, and Ours is the publication of the Agreement under Our name together with the entry that records Your acceptance in the public register at https://github.com/kleidos-dev/cla. The entry states the account, the version and SHA-256 digest of the text accepted and the date and time of the acceptance, and We show it to You as soon as it is made.

1.6 If an entry in the register names Your account and You did not make that acceptance, tell Us at mail@kleidos.dev. We will look into it with You and remove the entry if the acceptance cannot be confirmed.

2.4 Time and territory. The licence lasts for the whole legal term of the rights in each Contribution, including any extension, and covers every country in the world.

2.5 Joint works. If the Material is a work made in collaboration, You consent to its disclosure, modification and use by Us as the Agreement allows.

4.1 The Agreement sets the terms for the Contributions You choose to Submit. It does not cover the works You may create in the future as a whole. It does not oblige You to create or Submit anything, and it does not limit what You do with Your own work: You remain free to use and license each Contribution as You wish.

4.2 The licences for each Contribution are granted at the moment You Submit it, on the terms of the version of the Agreement that You accepted. Each Submission from Your account confirms that grant, and We accept it in writing through the record that the repository keeps of the Submission.

4.3 A work that does not exist when You accept the Agreement is licensed only if and when You Submit it.

4.4 A new version of the Agreement applies only to the Contributions You Submit after You accept it. Earlier Contributions stay under the version You had accepted when You Submitted them.

8.1 You consent in advance to the transfer of Our position under the Agreement, as a whole, to: (a) a legal entity that takes over the care of Kleidos, such as a company or foundation set up or designated by Us; (b) whoever acquires the Kleidos project or business; and (c) Our heirs. 9.1 The licences for a Contribution, once granted, cannot be revoked, except in the cases the Agreement states for the patent licence and those the law imposes. The Agreement's reference to its section "Term" is read with this clause.

9.2 You may stop contributing at any time, and You may tell Us in writing that the Agreement will not apply to Your later Submissions. This does not affect Contributions already Submitted.

9.3 If the Agreement ends for any reason, every licence and sublicence that We or Our licensees have already granted, open-source or not, stays in force.

This does not limit clause 2.1(b).
